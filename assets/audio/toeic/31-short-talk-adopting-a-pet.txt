I'm glad you're interested in adopting a pet.
The process is pretty simple.
First, you'll fill out an application form where we ask about your lifestyle and preferences.
Then, we'll review it
and match you with a pet that fits your home and needs.
Once we have a match,
we'll arrange a meet-and-greet to see if it's a good fit.
If everything goes well,
you can take your new friend home after completing some paperwork.
