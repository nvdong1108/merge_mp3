What you see before you is the Great Wall of China,
a monumental feat of ancient engineering.
Stretching over 13,000 miles,
it was built over several dynasties to protect against invasions.
Construction began in the 7th century BC
and continued for nearly 2,000 years.
The wall is made of various materials,
including brick, tamped earth, and stone.
Hiking along its path offers breathtaking views and a sense of stepping back in time.
